# hello, beat · 你好，節拍

最小、但完整的一支片：一個點跟著一首歌動，畫面自己說明每個動作是被什麼帶動的。
27 秒、4 個鏡頭、3 個音效，音樂由本專案的程式生成，可以自由使用。

The smallest complete film: one dot follows one song, and the screen shows what drives each
motion. 27 seconds, 4 shots, 3 sounds; the music is generated by this repository and free to use.

![hello, beat: the build, the stop and the drop](../../docs/media/hello-beat.gif)

## 它示範什麼 · What it shows

| 畫面 · picture | 由什麼帶動 · driven by | 鏡頭 · shot |
|---|---|---|
| 點落在第 1 小節，壓扁再回彈 · the dot lands on bar 1, squashes, wobbles | `song.T(1)`、`spring()` | `dot` |
| 每個大鼓脈動一次 · a pulse on every kick | `song.hit('kick', t, 0.09)`（分析出的大鼓 · analysed kicks） | `dot` |
| 小鼓彈出波紋 · every snare flicks a ring | `song.events('snare')` | `dot` |
| 20 格刻度隨滾奏點亮 · twenty ticks lit by the snare roll | 小節格線 · the bar grid `song.T(5, q, x)` | `dot` |
| 停頓：只剩外框、儀表隱藏 · the stop: an outline, no read-out | `song.isStop(t)`、`song.stops` | `dot`、`hud` |
| 爆開成紅色 · bursts open into red | `song.events('impact')` | `chorus` |
| 字一小節換一種語言 · one language per bar | `song.position(t).bar` | `chorus` |
| 收回、成為句點 · folds back, becomes the full stop | `song.T(11)`、`song.T(13)` | `chorus`、`end` |
| 下方時間軸 · the strip at the bottom | 整份 songmap：拍、小節、大鼓、停頓、重擊 · the whole songmap | `hud` |

## 自己做一次 · Make it yourself

歌不在 repo 裡（`audio/` 不進版控），幾秒就能重新生成。在 repo 根目錄：
The song is not in the repository (`audio/` is ignored); it is regenerated in seconds. From the
repository root:

```bash
node tools/studio.mjs music examples/hello-beat/audio/song.wav --plan-file examples/hello-beat/music.json --seed 7 --truth examples/hello-beat/data/song.truth.json --stems examples/hello-beat/audio/stems
```

```bash
node tools/studio.mjs serve examples/hello-beat
```

```bash
node tools/studio.mjs deliver examples/hello-beat
```

`serve` 開預覽（空白鍵播放，`[` `]` 跳小節，`L` 循環目前的鏡頭）；`deliver` 會混音、渲染、品管，
寫出 `out/hello-beat.mp4` 與 `DELIVERY.md`。`data/songmap.json` 已經在 repo 裡；
要重新分析：`node tools/studio.mjs analyze examples/hello-beat --stems examples/hello-beat/audio/stems`。

`serve` opens the preview (Space plays, `[` `]` jump bars, `L` loops the shot); `deliver` mixes,
renders and checks, and writes `out/hello-beat.mp4` and `DELIVERY.md`. `data/songmap.json` is
committed; the `analyze` line above rebuilds it.

## 兩個誠實的發現 · Two honest findings

生成器會寫下每一拍、每一下鼓的真實位置（`data/song.truth.json`），所以分析結果可以對答案：

1. **段落**：拍子誤差 ≤ 5.3 ms，停頓與重擊都找到了（誤差 ≤ 5 ms），但 12 小節被併成 3 段——
   短於 4 小節的段落會被合併。所以這支片用 `music.json` 的小節來命名段落，時間仍然來自量到的拍子。
2. **鼓的事件**：從整首混音分析，小鼓常被當成大鼓（大鼓多 9 個假的、小鼓多 36 個）；
   用分軌（`--stems`）分析：大鼓 30/32、小鼓 20/32，沒有假的。脈動用的是分軌的結果。

The generator writes down where every beat and drum hit really is (`data/song.truth.json`), so
the analysis can be graded: beats within 5.3 ms and the stop and impact within 5 ms, but the
12 bars were merged into 3 sections (sections under 4 bars are merged), so the film names its
parts by the bars of `music.json`. From the full mix, snares were often heard as kicks (9 false
kicks, 36 false snares); from stems, 30 of 32 kicks and 20 of 32 snares with none false.

## 試試看 · Try

- 把 `music.json` 的 `"bpm": 120` 改成 90，重新生成、分析：不用改任何程式，整支片跟著新的速度走
  （已測試）。Change the tempo to 90 and regenerate: the film follows without a code change (tested).
- 換一個 `--seed`：鼓和旋律會變，時間結構不變。Another seed changes the drums and melody, not the timing.
- 換成你自己的歌：放進 `audio/`，`studio analyze`，再改 `PARTS` 裡的小節。
  Your own song: put it in `audio/`, run `studio analyze`, then edit the bars in `PARTS`.

> 代理聽不到聲音。這支片的音量與峰值是量測值（−14.0 LUFS、−1.6 dBTP），之後由人聽過、認可（2026-10-24）。你的片發布前也一定要有人聽。
> An agent cannot hear: the loudness was measured, then a person listened to the film and approved it
> (2026-10-24). Yours needs the same before it goes out.
